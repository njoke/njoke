## QA to TPM: The Through-Line

Quality engineering taught a lesson most project managers miss: delivery risk lives in the details. After 15+ years of building test infrastructure, leading QA engineers, and owning release readiness across regulated industries — FinTech, election systems, enterprise SaaS — the move into Technical Project Management carried all of it forward.

That background informs every program decision today. Integration risks surface earlier. Dependencies get mapped before they become blockers. Go/No-Go reviews have the technical context they need. The instinct is the same; the scope is the program.

## What I Drive

- **Program delivery** — Lead the full lifecycle from discovery through post-launch across web, mobile, and API platforms.
- **Risk management** — Identify technical and delivery risk early, define mitigation plans, and keep stakeholders ahead of it.
- **Release readiness** — Define and enforce quality gates, coordinate go/no-go decisions, and manage deployment risk across teams.
- **Cross-functional alignment** — Bridge engineering, product, and business with shared context and explicit tradeoffs.
- **Metrics and visibility** — Define KPI frameworks — defect density, velocity, escape rate, customer effort score — that give leadership real signal.
- **AI program delivery** — Manage development and validation of AI and LLM features using structured evaluation frameworks.
- **Technical translation** — Convert ambiguous requirements into actionable specifications, acceptance criteria, and test strategies.
- **Hands-on tooling** — Build automation frameworks and AI testing tools as personal projects; understand how engineers think because the work came first.
